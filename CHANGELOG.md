## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added

- AmneziaWG VPN bar widget with profile management and connection toggle.
- Panel with AmneziaWG status, profile list, and vpn:// key import.
- WireGuard/AmneziaWG config parsing with obfuscation, padding, and header protection parameters.
- Multi-profile storage under `~/.config/omnezia/profiles/`.
